What the feed contains

  • Papers posted in the last two weeks on LingBuzz, Zenodo (the LingFeed, Linguistics and Language Science Press communities), PsyArXiv and SocArXiv, newest first.
  • New versions of papers, marked “Updated:”.
  • arXiv is not included, as it posts about 200 papers a day in Computation and Language. Browse it on LingFeed instead.

The feed is refreshed about every hour. Clicks on its links count toward LingFeed’s Most clicked list, just like clicks on the site.

What is a feed reader?

A feed reader is an app or website that collects new posts from the sites you follow in one place. Popular ones include NetNewsWire (free, for Mac and iPhone), Feedly and Inoreader (both on the web and phones). In most of them you choose “Add feed” or “Subscribe” and paste the address above. Many can also find the feed if you just paste LingFeed’s own address.

Some feed readers can also send new items to you by email.
